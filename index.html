Nothing here is measured from a real person. Joint angles start from approximate adult walking curves and change with the pattern and sliders. Computed values are model estimates. The model has no forces, so loads come from research and show direction only. Its rigid foot stays more flexed upward in swing than in real walking so the toes clear the floor.

Tap where it hurts on the body, or choose an area. The suggestions screen for warning signs, age and health conditions first. They are not a diagnosis.
